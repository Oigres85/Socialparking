## Description

Tired of circling the block? With Social Parking, drivers who are leaving a spot alert the drivers looking for one. Real time, free, no sign-up.

🚗 LEAVING?
Tap "Free my spot" and nearby drivers get notified instantly. Social Parking remembers where you parked and, when you walk back to your car, reminds you to share the spot.

🔍 SEARCHING?
Turn on search and watch spots open up around you on the map. Get a notification the moment one frees up.

✅ BOOK WITH ONE TAP
Book the spot and swap a 4-letter code: the leaving driver waits for you.

🏆 HELPING PAYS OFF
Earn points, level up from Pedestrian to Street Legend, unlock badges and see how many minutes you saved other drivers.

📣 THE MORE OF US, THE MORE SPOTS
Invite friends, coworkers and neighbours with your personal code: +25 points for every friend who joins.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
No account, no email, no phone number. Location is used only while you use the app. Delete all your data with one tap.

Please drive safely: use the app only when your vehicle is stopped.
